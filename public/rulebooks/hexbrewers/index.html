CHAPTER 01

Object of the Ritual

Become Ashenveil's most renowned hexbrewer by finishing nine rounds with more Prestige than every rival.

Each round you draw ingredients from a concealed bag and place them along your crucible spiral. Better ingredients carry you farther, improve future draws, and create powerful combinations. Voidshards increase pressure. Stop in time and claim the full value of your brew—or push once too often and shatter the crucible.

“A cautious brew feeds the living. A perfect brew convinces the dead to wait.”— Morcant's Seventh Margin
DRAWReveal one unknown ingredient from your bag.
PLACEAdvance along the spiral by its printed value.

CHAPTER 02

Your Instruments

The digital crucible performs the bookkeeping, but every instrument still obeys a physical rule.

Brewing Bag

Contains every ingredient available to be drawn this round. Its contents remain hidden.

Cursed Vial

Once per round, return the most recently drawn white Voidshard—unless that draw already shattered the crucible.

Rubies

Spend two after the market to refill the Vial or advance the starting droplet one space.

Rat Stone

From round two onward, trailing brewers receive a temporary head start based on rat tails.

CHAPTER 03

Preparing the Crucible

  1. 01

    Seat the brewers

    Choose two to four total brewers. The human takes one seat and AI rivals occupy the remaining seats.

  2. 02

    Choose a recipe set

    Select one shared recipe-book set. Every ingredient of the same color uses that set's effect for this match.

  3. 03

    Prepare equal bags

    Each brewer receives the same nine starting ingredients, one ruby, and a filled Cursed Vial.

  4. 04

    Place the droplets

    Every droplet begins at the starting space. The randomly chosen starting seat rotates clockwise after each round.

  5. 05

    Shuffle the Omens

    Twenty-four core Omen Cards form the deck. Veilglass Distillation adds four more. Reveal one at the beginning of each round without replacement.

CHAPTER 05

Reading the Omens

One Omen is revealed without replacement at the beginning of every round. It affects every brewer, but not every Omen resolves at the same moment.

BEFORE BREWING

Opening Portents

Apply immediate gifts, payments, ingredient choices, droplet movement, and rat-tail changes before the first draw.

DURING BREWING

Lingering Curses

Keep the card visible. It may alter movement, return a Voidshard, protect rewards, or offer a second chance after five placements.

DURING EVALUATION

Final Reckonings

Resolve bonuses for colors, ruby spaces, survival, pressure, or the final shape of the brew at the printed scoring step.

HOW TO READ A CARD

The lore warns. The effect commands.

  1. Read the title and omen-lore to identify the revealed card.
  2. Read the mechanical effect in full before any brewer acts.
  3. Resolve required choices in rotating seat order. The round waits until every choice is complete.
  4. Leave the Omen visible until evaluation finishes.
WHEN AN OMEN CONFLICTS

The Omen's specific instruction overrides the ordinary rule only for the stated duration. It does not permanently alter bags, recipe books, or pressure unless it explicitly says so.

The Complete Omen Archive

Twenty-four core Omens form the base deck. When Veilglass Afflictions is active, shuffle in the four marked Veilglass Omens before round one.

CHAPTER 06

Drawing and Placement

A token advances your position by its printed value. It is placed on the destination space; crossed spaces remain empty.

  • Resolve immediate effects as soon as the token lands.
  • A token placed by another effect resolves its own immediate effect before play continues.
  • Returned tokens leave empty distance. Later tokens never slide backward.
  • You may stop after every completed draw unless an effect states otherwise.

CHAPTER 08

Voidshard Pressure & Shattering

7SAFE

Only white Voidshards add pressure. Their printed values are added together.

Seven is safe. The instant the total exceeds seven, the crucible shatters and brewing ends. The final Voidshard remains placed.

CHAPTER 10

The Black Market

Spend the coins earned by your scoring space to permanently improve your bag.

  • Buy no more than two ingredients per round.
  • If buying two, they must be different colors.
  • Stock is shared and never replenished.
  • Unspent coins normally vanish at the end of the round.
  • Purchased ingredients enter your bag for future rounds.

FINITE SHARED STOCK

Starting ingredients are removed from the shared supply before play. Purchases, Omen rewards, trades, and upgrades all use that same stock. An unavailable ingredient cannot be taken by any effect unless that effect explicitly creates it.

CHAPTER 11

The Ninth Round & Victory

After the ninth evaluation, the brewer with the highest Prestige wins.

5UNSPENT COINS→1 PRESTIGE
2RUBIES→1 PRESTIGE

Tied leaders compare their final-round scoring spaces. If those are also tied, they share the victory. At the beginning of round six, every brewer receives one additional value-1 Voidshard.

Unused seals do not carry into another match. A seal can only be spent during its family's timing window.

THE FIFTH CHAIR

One more brewer may enter.

Mirewrought Pacts raises the table limit from four to five brewers and permits The Hollow to occupy the fourth rival seat.

ABYSSAL RUNOFF

The crucible's end is no longer the end.

The ingredient that reaches the final crucible space is placed normally. Every later draw enters the Runoff: its colored action does not activate, but half the total printed value of all Runoff ingredients, rounded down, becomes Prestige during evaluation.

MIRE INGREDIENTS

Echo Reed and Embercore

Echo Reed fixes its context-dependent movement when drawn and is shared by both optional archives. Embercore is a safe value-6 Brimstone exclusive to Mirewrought Pacts. Recipe sets 5 and 6 are also exclusive to Mirewrought Pacts.

THE INDEX OF TERMS

Glossary

When the Codex uses a precise term, it always means the same thing.

Adjacent rival
The brewer seated immediately beside you. In a duel, the other brewer is your only adjacent rival.
Bag
Your hidden personal supply of ingredients available to draw during brewing.
Distillate
Progress generated during Distillation and read by an Affliction Contract. It is not a spendable currency.
Droplet
Your permanent starting marker on the spiral. Its position persists between rounds.
Head start
Temporary movement applied before the first draw of a later brew. It does not move the droplet unless stated.
Immediate effect
An ingredient instruction resolved as soon as that ingredient is placed.
Placed ingredient
An ingredient occupying a spiral space. Returned, sampled-but-rejected, and reserved ingredients are not placed.
Pressure
The sum of values on placed Voidshards. Seven is safe; eight or more shatters the crucible.
Rat stone
A temporary round-start marker placed ahead of the droplet according to rat tails.
Recipe set
The shared collection that defines powers and costs for variable colored ingredients.
Scoring space
The first empty spiral space directly after the last placed ingredient.
Shatter
The immediate end of brewing caused when Voidshard pressure exceeds the current safe limit.

Common mistakes

  • Ruby rewards are checked on the empty scoring space, not beneath the last ingredient.
  • A Voidshard that causes shattering cannot be returned with the Cursed Vial.
  • Two market purchases cannot share a color.
  • Ingredient effects do not normally change the pressure contributed by Voidshards.
  • Rat-tail movement is temporary; droplet movement is permanent.
